# Quickstart

Get from sign-up to your first sent campaign in under 20 minutes.

<Steps>

### 1. Sign up and create a workspace

Head to [app.moosewave.com/signup](https://app.moosewave.com/signup) and create your account. You'll be prompted to name your **workspace** — this is your top-level tenant. Most users will only ever have one. Agencies and multi-publication teams can create more later.

<Callout type="info">
</Callout>

### 2. Migrate from your current platform

From the workspace home, click **Migrate**. Pick your source (Mailchimp, ConvertKit, Substack, Beehiiv, MailerLite, or CSV). Moosewave will run a **dry-run** first — pulling counts and a verification report without writing anything — so you can confirm the import shape before committing.

<Callout type="info">
</Callout>

See [Migration overview](/migration) for the full lifecycle and what each source does.

### 3. Verify deliverability

After migration, Moosewave's [Inbox Reality](/deliverability) dashboard runs the 5-layer audit on your sender domain: SPF, DKIM, DMARC, BIMI, and a seedlist test. Anything failing shows up as a ranked fix from the [AI Diagnostician](/deliverability/diagnostician). Most setups pass within minutes; DNS-related fixes propagate in 5–60 min.

<Callout type="info">
</Callout>

### 4. Generate your first sequence

Open **Voice AI → New sequence**. Moosewave will offer to build a [voice profile](/voice-ai/profile) from the campaigns it just imported (no extra work — those count as a source). Pick a [template](/voice-ai/templates) — `welcome-5-email` is a great starter — set your variables, and generate. Each email comes with a [fidelity score](/voice-ai/fidelity-scoring); regenerate any that score below 60.

<Callout type="info">
</Callout>

### 5. Send your first campaign

From **Campaigns → New**, write or paste your first broadcast, pick a segment, preview, and send. If you want a safety net, run it through the [Pre-Send Predictor](/deliverability/predictor) for an inbox-placement estimate before launch.

<Callout type="info">
</Callout>

</Steps>
