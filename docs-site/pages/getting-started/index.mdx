# Getting Started

Moosewave is an email platform built for solo operators, indie newsletter writers, course creators, and small media teams who have outgrown — or been outpriced by — Mailchimp, ConvertKit, Substack, and Beehiiv.

## Who Moosewave is for

- You write to **2k–500k subscribers** and your ESP bill keeps climbing.
- You spend more time fighting your ESP than writing.
- You've shelved a sequence project because "set up automations" sat on your todo list for 6 months.
- You want **inbox placement you can measure**, not promised.

## The 20-minute promise

If you've used a modern ESP, the path from sign-up to your first sent campaign on Moosewave should take ~20 minutes:

1. Sign up and verify your sender domain (5 min, mostly DNS propagation).
2. Migrate your list from your current ESP — dry-run, review, commit (5 min for most accounts).
3. Verify your deliverability passes (~2 min — automatic).
4. Generate a welcome sequence from your voice fingerprint (5 min).
5. Send your first campaign (3 min).

## Where to start

If you're brand new, follow the [Quickstart](/getting-started/quickstart). If you're evaluating, jump into [Migration](/migration) — it's where Moosewave's value proposition is most concrete.
