# Opt-in: single vs double

Every Moosewave form picks one of two opt-in modes. The choice has real implications for list quality and deliverability.

## Single opt-in

The submitter enters their email and is **immediately** added to your list as `subscribed`. They can unsubscribe via every email's unsubscribe link.

**When to use:**

- High-trust contexts: your own product onboarding, your existing-customer database.
- When you have strong [spam protection](/forms/spam-protection) (honeypot + reCAPTCHA) and confidence the form isn't being scraped/spammed.

**Risks:**

- Bad actors can sign up other people's emails (subscription bombing).
- Mistyped emails become hard bounces or — worse — land at the wrong real person.
- Deliverability impact: a single bad email harvested into your list costs you reputation.

## Double opt-in (recommended for most cases)

The submitter enters their email and is added as `pending`. They receive a **confirmation email** with a unique tokenised link. Clicking the link flips them to `subscribed`.

**When to use:**

- Public forms where anyone on the internet could submit.
- Any form embedded on your blog / homepage / public landing page.
- GDPR/CASL contexts where explicit opt-in is required.
- Any time list quality matters more than top-of-funnel volume (almost always).

**Tradeoffs:**

- Conversion drops 10–30% (depending on industry, audience). Some submitters never confirm.
- The lost confirms were almost all noise — dropped emails, accidental submits, abandoned-tab signups.

<Callout type="info">
**Default to double opt-in.** The single opt-in lift is rarely worth the deliverability and list-hygiene cost. Moosewave defaults forms to double opt-in for this reason.
</Callout>

## The confirmation token flow

When a submitter signs up to a double-opt-in form:

1. Moosewave creates a `pending` subscriber record.
2. A confirmation token (32-byte URL-safe random) is generated and stored hashed.
3. A confirmation email is sent immediately. The link looks like:
   ```
   https://forms.moosewave.com/confirm?t=<token>
   ```
4. On click, Moosewave verifies the token, flips the subscriber to `subscribed`, fires the `subscriber.confirmed` [webhook](/webhooks/events), and shows your configured thank-you page.
5. Tokens expire after **14 days**. Unconfirmed pending subscribers are auto-purged after 30 days.

## Customising the confirmation email

Under **Forms → \<form\> → Confirmation email**:

- **Subject line** — defaults to `Confirm your subscription to {workspace_name}`.
- **Body** — MJML editor. The default is intentionally plain ("Click to confirm") because plain confirmation emails have the highest click-through.
- **From identity** — defaults to the workspace's primary [sender identity](/getting-started/account-setup); overridable.
- **Confirmation button text** — default "Confirm subscription".
- **Post-confirm redirect** — URL to send the subscriber to after they click confirm.

<Callout type="warning">
**Don't oversell the confirmation email.** Treating it as a marketing email ("Welcome! Here's everything we do!") drops confirm-rates significantly. Keep it minimal — confirm + done. Save the welcome content for the actual welcome email triggered by `subscriber.confirmed`.
</Callout>
