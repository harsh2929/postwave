# Voice profile

A voice profile is the structured artifact built from your [sources](/voice-ai/sources). It's the *contract* that generation conditions on.

## What's inside

A profile has six sections:

### 1. Stylistic

Quantitative measurements over your source corpus:

- **Sentence length**: median, p10, p90.
- **Paragraph length** (in sentences): median, p10, p90.
- **Average words per email** (or post-shaped unit).
- **Reading level** (Flesch-Kincaid grade equivalent).
- **Punctuation density**: em-dash use, parentheticals, ellipses.

### 2. Vocabulary

- **Recurring high-frequency words** — words you use more than baseline by ≥3×.
- **Signature phrases** — n-grams (2–5 word) that appear in ≥10% of your samples.
- **Avoided words** — common English words you use less than baseline by ≥3× (often a stronger voice signal than your favourites).
- **Domain vocabulary** — terms specific to your newsletter's subject area.

### 3. Structural

- **Use of bold/italic**: rate per 100 words.
- **Use of lists**: ordered vs unordered ratio.
- **Use of subheaders**: per-piece average count, hierarchy depth.
- **Link density**: links per 500 words.
- **Use of pull-quotes / blockquotes**.

### 4. Tonal

A 5-dimensional vector inferred by an LLM judge over a sample of your content:

- **Formal ↔ Casual**
- **Warm ↔ Direct**
- **Serious ↔ Playful**
- **Concrete ↔ Abstract**
- **Hedged ↔ Assertive**

Each dimension is a single float -1.0 to +1.0 with a confidence interval.

### 5. Persona

- **Point of view**: 1st-person singular / editorial-we / 2nd-person-imperative / 3rd-person.
- **Self-disclosure level**: low / medium / high.
- **Expertise framing**: peer / teacher / guide / reporter.
- **Humour register**: dry / warm / absurd / earnest / none.

### 6. Representative samples

3–5 short excerpts from your source corpus that the profile-builder selected as the most concentrated voice signals. These are passed verbatim into generation prompts as anchors.

## When to rebuild

- **After a major writing-style shift** (you started writing more personal, you switched topics, you brought on a co-author).
- **Quarterly** as a hygiene rebuild — slow drift accumulates.
- **After ingesting a new source type** (e.g., you added podcast transcripts; rebuild to incorporate the new spoken cadence signals).

Rebuilds are non-destructive — old profiles are versioned and you can roll generation back to a prior profile if a rebuild changed the voice unfavourably.

<Callout type="warning">
Rebuilds are not free of compute — they take 2–10 minutes depending on source volume. Don't rebuild reflexively after every new tweet.
</Callout>
