# Voice sources

A voice profile is only as good as the content you feed it. Moosewave supports five source types. **More is better, but quality matters more than quantity** — feeding a single great essay beats feeding a hundred lukewarm tweets.

## The 5 source types

| Source             | Ideal volume         | Minimum useful  | What it captures                              |
| ------------------ | -------------------- | --------------- | --------------------------------------------- |
| **Past campaigns** | 20+ campaigns        | 5               | Email-shaped voice — the closest match.       |
| **Tweets / X posts** | 500+ posts          | 100             | Concise voice, hooks, humour register.        |
| **Blog posts**     | 10+ long-form posts  | 3               | Argument structure, paragraph cadence.        |
| **Podcast transcripts** | 5+ episodes      | 2               | Spoken cadence, conversational fillers.       |
| **LinkedIn posts** | 100+ posts           | 20              | Professional register, hook patterns.         |
| **Paste-in fallback** | 3,000+ words       | 3,000 words     | When you have nothing else.                   |

## How to add each

### Past campaigns

If you migrated via [Mailchimp](/migration/mailchimp), [ConvertKit](/migration/convertkit), or any source that brought your campaign archive — they're already attached. Moosewave auto-suggests them when you build a profile.

For sources without API access (Substack), upload the `.eml` or HTML files manually under **Voice AI → Sources → Add campaigns**.

### Tweets / X posts

Two ways:

1. **X data export** — request your archive at [x.com/settings/your_archive](https://x.com/settings/your_archive). Drop the `.zip` into Moosewave; we extract `tweets.js`.
2. **Public scrape (limited)** — provide your handle; we pull the most recent 500 public posts via the X API.

Replies and quote-tweets are excluded by default (they're often reactive, not voice-representative). Toggleable.

### Blog posts

- **RSS feed URL** — Moosewave fetches the full feed and ingests posts.
- **Direct upload** — paste URLs or upload `.md` / `.html` files.
- **WordPress export** — drop a `WordPress.xml` file.

### Podcast transcripts

- Paste a transcript directly (Otter.ai, Descript, Riverside exports work).
- Provide an `.mp3` URL — Moosewave runs Whisper transcription server-side (~$0.30 per hour of audio, billed at cost).

<Callout type="info">
Podcast transcripts add **spoken cadence** to your profile — useful if you want your emails to sound less corporate. If you don't podcast, skip; the other sources cover everything else.
</Callout>

### LinkedIn posts

- **LinkedIn data export** — request from `linkedin.com/mypreferences/d/download-my-data`. Drop `Shares.csv`.
- We don't scrape LinkedIn (their TOS prohibits it).

### Paste-in fallback

If you have nothing else (brand-new newsletter), paste 3,000+ words of your writing — anything from old emails to a notes app dump. Below 3,000 words, Moosewave will refuse to build a profile; the result would be too generic.

## Volume guidelines

For a *strong* profile across all dimensions:

- **20+ campaigns** OR **10+ blog posts** OR **(5 campaigns + 200 tweets + 5 LinkedIn)** is the sweet spot.
- Single-source profiles (only tweets, only LinkedIn) work but skew tonally — Moosewave will warn.
- Refreshing the profile monthly with the latest content keeps it from drifting from current you.

## Privacy

All source content is stored encrypted at rest, scoped to your workspace, and **never used to train shared models or other tenants' profiles**. See the [FAQ](/faq) for the full data-use posture.
