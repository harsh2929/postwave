# Moosewave Documentation

Welcome. Moosewave is the modern home for newsletter operators who want to migrate off legacy ESPs in an afternoon, land in the inbox by default, and ship sequences that actually sound like them.

These docs are organised around three pillars and the surfaces that wrap them.

## The three pillars

<div style={{ display: 'grid', gridTemplateColumns: 'repeat(auto-fit, minmax(240px, 1fr))', gap: '1rem', marginTop: '1.5rem' }}>

### [Migration →](/migration)

Move from Mailchimp, ConvertKit, Substack, Beehiiv, MailerLite, or a CSV. Source-supported subscribers, tags, segments, automations, archive, and source-specific billing evidence are dry-runnable with a 7-day conflict-aware rollback window. Unsupported resources remain explicit rebuild evidence; Substack tiers are metadata and do not migrate live Stripe/Apple billing.

### [Deliverability →](/deliverability)

Inbox Reality, our 5-layer deliverability stack: SPF/DKIM/DMARC/BIMI validation, controlled-inbox observations when a deployment has an approved active roster, an AI Diagnostician that proposes ranked fixes, and a Pre-Send Predictor that scores campaigns before you hit send.

### [Voice AI →](/voice-ai)

Build a fingerprint of how you write from your past campaigns, tweets, blog posts, podcast transcripts, and LinkedIn. Generate sequences from 50 templates with per-email fidelity scoring.
