# Migrating from Substack

Moosewave imports the publication data that Substack places in its user-owned
CSV/ZIP exports. The dry-run keeps source status, membership tier, and signup
date distinct from two things the export cannot prove: portable email consent
and live billing continuity.

<Callout type="warning">
This migration does **not** move, cancel, recreate, or attach live paid
subscriptions. Paid, founding, gift, and comp values are membership metadata,
not Stripe/Apple billing objects or Moosewave entitlements. Coordinate the
billing handoff with Substack and the payment provider before changing the
source publication.
</Callout>

## Step 1 — Create the source export

For subscribers plus post history, use Substack's publication export:

1. Open the publication dashboard.
2. Go to **Settings → Exports**.
3. Select **Create new export**.
4. Download the ZIP when Substack finishes preparing it.

Substack describes this ZIP as containing the publication's posts, subscriber
list, and related statistics. Upload the original ZIP without opening and
re-saving its CSV files. See [Substack's publication-export
instructions](https://support.substack.com/hc/en-us/articles/360037466012-How-do-I-export-my-posts).

If you need a subscriber-only snapshot, open **Subscribers → All subscribers →
… → Export** and choose **All columns**. Substack says visible-only exports can
contain a selected subset of columns, while all-column exports can include
activity and plan details. See [Substack's current subscriber-export
instructions](https://support.substack.com/hc/en-us/articles/6314498343700-How-do-I-export-my-email-list-on-Substack).

## Step 2 — Upload one ZIP or CSV

From **Migrate → Substack**, upload either:

- the complete Substack ZIP (preferred); or
- one subscriber CSV with an email column.

For a ZIP, Moosewave safely extracts portable CSV/HTML files, rejects path
traversal, symbolic links, ambiguous subscriber lists, and oversized expanded
archives, then locates:

- `email_list.*.csv` or an equivalent subscriber CSV;
- `posts.csv`;
- `posts/<post_id>.*.html` content fragments; and
- optional `<post_id>.delivers.csv` / `<post_id>.opens.csv` aggregate evidence.

Recipient identities inside delivery/open files are counted and discarded.
They are not copied into campaign analytics.

## Step 3 — Review the dry-run

The report shows:

- de-duplicated email identities;
- disabled, unsubscribed, bounced, and complaint state where exported;
- paid, founding, gift, comp, and free membership metadata;
- email-sent posts with matching HTML content;
- aggregate delivery/open evidence when present; and
- a fixed checklist for every non-portable Substack surface.

Duplicate rows merge conservatively: a complaint, bounce, unsubscribe, or
disabled row wins over an earlier active row. If the export has no status
column, the subscriber remains unconfirmed.

<Callout type="info">
Substack signup dates are stored as `substack_created_at` provenance. They are
not written as consent timestamps. Even a source row marked active remains
unconfirmed in Moosewave until reviewed permission evidence authorizes sending
from the destination.
</Callout>

## Step 4 — Handle paid readers outside the file import

Substack can collect payment through Stripe and through Apple in-app purchase.
A connected Stripe account can also contain activity unrelated to the
publication. Therefore an arbitrary Stripe account ID or a matching email is
not sufficient proof of publication, product, customer, entitlement, or tax
ownership.

Before cutover:

1. Inventory Stripe-billed and Apple-billed readers separately.
2. Bind every Stripe product/price/customer set to this publication.
3. Reconcile trials, renewal dates, founding plans, gifts, comps, pledges,
   refunds, disputes, taxes, and failed payments.
4. Decide how destination checkout and webhook ownership will work.
5. Ask Substack support for the correct move-away sequence before disconnecting
   or turning off paid subscriptions.
6. Test renewal and entitlement behavior in an isolated destination before
   announcing cutover.

Substack warns that turning off paid subscriptions can refund active
subscriptions, notify readers, cancel subscriptions, and revoke paid access;
its help article directs publications moving platforms to support. See
[Substack's paid-subscription shutdown guidance](https://support.substack.com/hc/en-us/articles/360060408872-How-do-I-turn-off-paid-subscriptions-on-Substack).

## What the adapter imports

| Source evidence | Moosewave result |
| --- | --- |
| Email identity | De-duplicated subscriber |
| Source email/subscription state | Namespaced provenance plus conservative destination status |
| Signup date | `substack_created_at` provenance, never portable consent |
| Free/paid/founding/gift/comp type | `substack_subscription_type` and `substack:tier:*` metadata |
| `posts.csv` row with email-send timestamp | Immutable sent campaign archive record |
| Matching `posts/<post_id>.*.html` | Archive HTML content |
| Delivery/open CSVs | Aggregate counts only; recipient identities discarded |
| ZIP file list and digest | Durable export-inventory evidence |

## Explicit rebuild or review surfaces

- Live Stripe or Apple billing, entitlements, checkout, webhooks, disputes, and
  tax continuity
- Consent proof, complaints/bounces not present in the selected export, and
  source deletions absent from the snapshot
- Drafts, pages, web-only/direct content without supported sent-email evidence
- Audience-specific/free/paid/founding content variants and paywall semantics
- Comments, reactions, moderation, Notes, restacks, follows, and chat
- Recommendations, referrals, and other network-growth relationships
- Welcome emails, scheduled direct emails, and automations
- Surveys and responses (Substack exposes responses through a separate export)
- Podcasts, private feeds, audio/video/live media, transcripts, and distribution
- Images, social thumbnails, files, and attachment ownership (HTML may retain
  remote Substack CDN URLs)
- Theme, About/custom pages, navigation, domain/redirects, SEO metadata, embeds,
  and capture widgets
- Per-reader delivery, open, view, click, activity, and targeting history
- Apps, analytics/pixels, webhooks, API clients, sender/DNS, and reply routing

## Troubleshooting

- **ZIP says no subscriber CSV:** generate the export from the publication that
  owns the audience. The ZIP must contain one unambiguous CSV with an email
  column.
- **Multiple subscriber CSVs:** upload the untouched official ZIP, or choose one
  current all-column subscriber CSV. Moosewave will not guess between equally
  likely audience files.
- **All active subscribers are unconfirmed:** expected. Attach reviewed consent
  evidence through the destination's consent workflow before enabling them.
- **Posts have no body:** the `posts.csv` row had no matching
  `posts/<post_id>.*.html` fragment. Regenerate the export or preserve the post
  manually.
- **ZIP exceeds 200 MB:** contact support for a controlled assisted transfer;
  do not split recipient activity files into ad-hoc subscriber imports.
- **Spreadsheet encoding or columns changed:** upload the original download.
  Spreadsheet tools can change UTF-8, timestamps, headers, and leading IDs.
