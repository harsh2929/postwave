# SPF — Sender Policy Framework

SPF lets a domain owner declare — via a DNS TXT record — which IP addresses are allowed to send mail "from" that domain. Receiving mailservers look up the SPF record and check the connecting IP against it.

## The mechanism

An SPF record is a single DNS TXT record at the apex of your domain (or a subdomain you send from):

```
yourdomain.com.   IN  TXT   "v=spf1 include:_spf.moosewave.com include:_spf.google.com -all"
```

Components:

- `v=spf1` — version. Always this.
- `include:<domain>` — delegate authorization to another domain's SPF record (e.g., Moosewave, Google Workspace, your transactional ESP).
- `ip4:1.2.3.4` / `ip6:...` — explicit IPs you also send from.
- `a` / `mx` — authorize the domain's `A` or `MX` records.
- `~all` — **softfail**: anything not listed is suspicious but should still be accepted.
- `-all` — **hardfail**: anything not listed should be rejected. **Recommended for established domains.**

## The 10-DNS-lookup limit

SPF allows a maximum of **10 DNS lookups** during evaluation (RFC 7208 §4.6.4). Every `include:`, `a`, `mx`, `ptr`, and `exists:` mechanism counts. If you exceed it, mailservers return `permerror` and most treat the message as if SPF failed entirely.

This is the **single most common SPF problem**. Each big SaaS sender's `include:` often expands into 2–4 nested lookups internally, so you can blow the limit with as few as 4–5 includes.

Moosewave's SPF validator counts the *real* lookup cost (recursing into your includes) and warns at 8 to give you a margin.

## Common mistakes

<Callout type="warning">
**Multiple SPF records.** A domain can have **only one** SPF TXT record. Two records = `permerror`. If you need to authorize multiple senders, merge them into one record with multiple `include:` mechanisms.
</Callout>

- **Using `+all`**: this authorises every IP on the internet to send as you. Never do this.
- **Forgetting the include for your transactional ESP**: order confirmations and password resets start landing in spam.
- **Hardfailing too early**: switch to `-all` only after you've watched DMARC aggregate reports for 1–2 weeks with no legitimate `softfail` traffic.
- **Subdomain confusion**: `mail.yourdomain.com` does NOT inherit SPF from `yourdomain.com`. If you send from a subdomain, it needs its own SPF record (or its own `include:`).

## What Moosewave's validator checks

Run from **Deliverability → SPF**:

1. **Record exists** at the sending domain.
2. **Exactly one** record (no duplicates).
3. **Includes Moosewave** (`_spf.moosewave.com`).
4. **Lookup count ≤ 10** (we count the real recursion, not just top-level mechanisms).
5. **Qualifier strength** — green for `-all`, yellow for `~all`, red for `+all` or no qualifier.
6. **No deprecated mechanisms** (`ptr` is allowed but penalised).

The validator runs on every page load and on a 6-hour background schedule. Failures trigger a [Diagnostician](/deliverability/diagnostician) entry with a ranked fix.
