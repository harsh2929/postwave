# Frequently asked questions

## Pricing & ownership

### What does Moosewave cost?

Only the current [pricing page](https://moosewave.com/pricing), checkout, and
executed order can state an offer. This archived documentation does not define
prices, included usage, taxes, discounts, plan limits, or availability.

### Do you offer refunds?

No fixed refund promise is made here. Eligibility, timing, exclusions, and the
request process must come from the approved Refund Policy and order presented
at checkout. Do not rely on historical support or documentation copy.

### Is there a lifetime deal (LTD)?

No lifetime-plan sale or universal continuity promise is approved by this
page. Any existing customer's rights come only from that customer's executed
order and applicable terms; availability of a future offer requires a separate
product, legal, tax, and billing approval.

### What happens if Moosewave shuts down?

Do not rely on an implied self-host distribution, escrow, permissive relicensing,
wind-down release, or hosted-service continuity commitment. None is currently
approved or evidenced. Data-export capabilities and any post-termination
window are governed by the deployed product and approved order/legal terms.
Customers that require escrow, continuity, or transition assistance must put
that scope in an executed agreement.

### Who owns my subscriber list?

Data rights, controller/processor roles, export availability, retention, and
deletion obligations are governed by the approved Terms, Privacy Policy, DPA,
and executed order. Confirm those documents and the deployed workflows for the
customer's jurisdiction before making a contractual statement.

## Privacy / compliance

### Are you GDPR-compliant?

Do not treat source code or this page as a blanket compliance certification.
Before a GDPR claim, verify the approved DPA and Privacy Policy, subprocessors,
lawful-basis and data-subject workflows, retention/deletion behavior,
international-transfer mechanism, and the live region/data-flow evidence. No
EU-only endpoint or residency commitment is established here.

### Are you SOC 2 / HIPAA / etc.?

The repository does not prove a completed SOC examination, HIPAA program, CCPA
attestation, or other certification. Do not claim a report, certification, or
regulated-workload eligibility until the responsible owner links a current,
independently issued artifact and approved customer terms.

### Do you train AI models on my data?

Tenant-isolation controls and provider configuration are implemented in code,
but a categorical no-training promise requires approved privacy terms,
provider contracts, retention settings, and live configuration evidence. Refer
customers to the current Privacy Policy and DPA rather than this archived page.

### Is Moosewave AGPL?

The application source under `apps/`, `services/`, and `infra/` is
AGPL-3.0-only. Each `packages/*` root is MIT licensed. Tuned assets under
`proprietary/` are separately controlled and excluded from public and customer
artifacts; MIT placeholders live under `proprietary-stubs/`. Moosewave uses a
separately running upstream AGPL Listmonk process and contains no Listmonk fork
or submodule. See [Source and independent operation](/self-host) and the root
`LICENSE-BOUNDARY.md`.

## Migration

### What sources do you support?

Mailchimp, ConvertKit (Kit), Substack, Beehiiv, MailerLite, and CSV. See [Migration](/migration). Adding new sources is a quarterly cadence; vote at our [public roadmap](https://moosewave.com/roadmap).

### How accurate is segment translation?

For tag-based and attribute-based conditions: 100%. For provider-specific stats (Mailchimp Member Rating, Beehiiv engagement composites): surfaced as unsupported with a manual recreation flow. See the [Verification report](/migration/verification-report).

### Will my paid Substack subscribers keep paying me?

The file import makes no automatic billing-continuity promise. It preserves
paid/founding/gift/comp membership metadata, but it does not move or mutate
Stripe or Apple subscriptions and does not create Moosewave entitlements. Review
the publication-bound billing handoff, including Substack's shutdown sequence,
with Substack and the payment provider. See [Substack migration](/migration/substack#step-4--handle-paid-readers-outside-the-file-import).

### Do you offer concierge migration?

Migration assistance is not automatically included by this page. It is
available only when the current offer or executed order states its scope,
provider coverage, evidence requirements, scheduling, and support terms.

## Sending

### Can I bring my own sender (BYOSP)?

Yes. Connect SES, Postmark, Sendgrid, Mailgun, or any SMTP relay. See [Sending API](/api-reference/sending).

### Can I pause sending?

Yes — the **Sending tier** is pause-anytime. Stop sending mid-month and your reputation infrastructure (warmup, IPs) stays in place for resume.

### Can I send from multiple sender domains?

Multiple-domain capability and limits depend on the deployed plan, provider,
and verified domain setup. Do not promise an unlimited allowance; confirm the
current entitlement and sending-provider capacity.

## Voice AI

### How is voice quality measured?

Per email, with a [4-component fidelity score](/voice-ai/fidelity-scoring). Scores below 60 auto-retry; final scores show as red/yellow/green badges in the sequence preview.

### Can I use my own LLM (OpenAI, Anthropic, local)?

Managed-provider availability is controlled by the deployed configuration and
approved plan. The repository does not currently offer a supported self-host
distribution or promise a portable `LLM_PROVIDER` switch. Operators building
from source must review and test the adapters and credentials they configure.

### How many templates ship with Moosewave?

50, hand-authored, across 11 categories. See [Templates](/voice-ai/templates).

## Operations

### Is there an agency / multi-publication mode?

The source includes organization/workspace capabilities. Availability, seat or
workspace limits, and billing behavior depend on the deployed plan and approved
order; this page does not grant an unlimited entitlement.

### Can I self-host?

There is no approved or supported self-host distribution, installer, price, or
customer image channel today. The source licenses remain effective, but
packaging, maintenance, support, and an SLA require an executed agreement tied
to a reviewed artifact. See [Source and independent operation](/self-host).

### What's your uptime?

No historical uptime or GA claim is established by the repository. The
production platform is not yet certified, and a public host name is not uptime
evidence. Quote only a live status history or an SLA in an executed order after
the production owner verifies it.

### Where are you on Listmonk?

Moosewave calls a separately running upstream Listmonk process over HTTP. This
repository contains no Listmonk source tree, fork, or submodule, and the local
development stack selects upstream v6.1.0 by default. A release must resolve an
approved immutable upstream image digest. The source and artifact boundary is
documented in `LICENSE-BOUNDARY.md`.
